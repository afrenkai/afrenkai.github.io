Hi, I'm Artem!

I am currently an MS student at NYU Courant's Data Science program. I received my BS in Data Science from Worcester Polytechnic Institute where I had the pleasure to work under Prof. Roee Shraga at the YOSSI Lab and also under Prof. Neil Heffernan at the ASSISTments Lab.

My research interests are representation learning, diffusion models, and stochastic processes. I am also interested in non-convex optimization, mechanistic interpretability, and non-autoregressive methods as a whole! I think that we leave a lot on the table focusing only on token/pixel space, and hope to explore that in my work. I am always on the lookout for research collaborations, so don't hesitate to send me an email.
